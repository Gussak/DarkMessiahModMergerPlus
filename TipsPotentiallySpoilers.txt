Fire source anywhere
- there is some movable furniture that can only be kicked, but can be put on fire several times, what is great for fire arrows!

- enable the developer GOD mode, and fly where you want to teleport (be sure to not trigger anything, the yellow regions)
- Use the Teleport Mark key
- move back where you were (better mark this place too)
